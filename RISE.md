# Saint Francis University RISE | Ground-Tier Research

**Role:** Student contributor to systems setup, integration, and field testing.

On November 20, 2026, our team configured a Raspberry Pi 5 for use with MagPi interface boards and an RM3100 magnetometer. We installed and verified Raspberry Pi OS, enabled I²C, compiled the `runMag` utility, confirmed sensor communication, and collected initial magnetic-field measurements at four possible field sites.

**Technical areas:** Linux system configuration, Raspberry Pi, I²C, hardware interfaces, compilation with `make`, troubleshooting, sensor data acquisition, and field documentation.

This is ongoing university research. The full field report, internal network settings, site information, and future deployment details are not published here pending approval from the project team.
